# Shia occasions research: months 10-12 (Shawwal, Dhu al-Qa'da, Dhu al-Hijja)

First pass. Mafatih / Haqibat / Iran official calendar / marja' calendars were NOT reachable via search or fetch; sources are WikiShia (ar/fa), Alkafeel forum reposts of Shia shrine publications (Shaaer bulletin, Imam Hussain Shrine library) and Persian Wikifeqh. Many WikiShia/Alkafeel pairs are weakly independent; medium confidence flagged where so.

## Problems
- Search tool returned few Shia primary pages; ar.wikishia month pages give no day numbers; Abd al-Azim al-Hasani page 504.
- Uhud 7 vs 15 Shawwal, Mubahala 23 vs 24, marriage 1 vs 6 Dhu al-Hijja recorded as main+alternate.
- Imam al-Jawad martyrdom is 'last day' of Dhu al-Qa'da (29 or 30).
