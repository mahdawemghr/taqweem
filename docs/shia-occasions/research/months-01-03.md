## Problems / caveats
- Haqibat al-Mu'min, Mafatih text and marja' calendar sites (sistani.org etc.) were not reachable/found via search; sources are WikiShia (ar, day pages), the Abbasid shrine (alkafeel) forum calendar posts (not scholarly, post details inconsistent), a few scholar-based WikiShia article citations (Tabarsi, Kulayni for Imam al-Ridha), and snn.ir. Therefore confidence is mostly "medium" where a forum is the second source. A second pass against Haqibat/Mafatih is recommended.
- alkafeel node/7109 and 49201 contain inconsistent day numbers (different posts disagree); only agreed items were used.
- Other opened pages: https://ar.wikishia.net/view/تصنيف:مناسبات_شهر_محرم ; https://ar.wikishia.net/view/عشرة_صفر ; https://ar.wikishia.net/view/الإمام_الحسن_المجتبى ; https://ar.wikishia.net/view/الإمام_الرضا_عليه_السلام ; https://fa.wikishia.net/view/نهم_ربیع‌الاول (via search summary).
